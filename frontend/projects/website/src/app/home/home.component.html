Why event sourcing?

Most systems only keep the latest version of their data, so the story of how it got there is lost. Event sourcing records every change as it happens, so you always know what happened, when, and why.

Complete audit trail

Every change is recorded and never overwritten, so you can always see who changed what, and when.

Time travel debugging

Replay the changes to see exactly how your data got into any state. Bugs become easy to follow.

Rebuild at any time

Because the full history is kept, you can rebuild your data, or build new views of it, whenever you need to.

Everything included

Replay, snapshots, evolving events and scaling are built in. You focus on your business logic, Eventify takes care of the rest.
