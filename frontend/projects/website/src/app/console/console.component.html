The questions it answers

When an aggregate looks wrong, you have questions. The console answers them from its own history.

What happened to this order?

Every event, in the order it happened, with its payload and metadata.

Can I try it again?

Replay an aggregate

Follow one order from start to finish: every event with the state it left behind, and every command with its outcome, including the one that failed and was retried.
